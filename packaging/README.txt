Endless Time Consuming Worlds - test build
==========================================

Thanks for testing!

Starting
--------
Run EndlessTimeConsumingWorlds.exe. Keep the whole folder together: the
game needs the files next to it.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the game isn't signed.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

The first time you open a world, it can take up to a minute to load while
your graphics driver prepares the game's shaders. After that it's quick.

What you need
-------------
- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- A graphics card with up-to-date drivers that support Vulkan (most cards
  from the last ten years do)

Playing
-------
- Worlds: pick one or make a new one. A new world can be Creative or Survival.
- In a world, press H to see the controls, and Esc for the menu (settings,
  the main menu, save and quit).

Your worlds are saved in the "saves" folder here. To keep them when you get
a newer build, copy that folder into the new one.

Reporting a problem
-------------------
Please send:
- what you were doing and what went wrong (a screenshot helps)
- log.txt from this folder
- the version from the main menu's bottom-right corner (also in version.txt)
